# Inside Your Body — content rules (read by the script-writing agent)

## Channel
English health channel. Animated explainers: kinetic typography + 2D motion graphics + 2D illustrations, AI narrator.
Audience: general adults worldwide. Tone: warm, energetic, exciting, never preachy, never fear-mongering for its own sake.

## Topic scope
Anything about health: how people stay healthy and what harms them. Everyday, relatable topics
(medicines, lifestyle, nutrition, sleep, mind, habits, myths, prevention, hygiene, the body).

## Long video structure (retention first) — target 8:10–8:40
The whole video is ONE story the viewer can't stop watching.
1. HOOK (0–15 s): a shocking line, question or mini-scene. No channel intro before it.
2. PROBLEM: make the viewer feel "this is MY problem" (everyday situations, second person "you").
3. EXPLANATION: what happens and why, told as a story (a character, a timeline, inside the body).
4. SOLUTION: practical, simple, safe steps the viewer can start today.
5. WRAP-UP: one-line takeaway + ask a question for comments + subscribe.
- Add 3–4 open loops / mid-video hooks ("but the worst part is still coming…", "in a minute I'll show you the one fix…").
- Change the visual idea every 6–12 seconds.
- Sentences short and spoken; energetic narrator delivery.

## Accuracy & safety
- Every number or claim must come from a reputable source (WHO, CDC, NHS, NIH/PubMed, major medical bodies). List sources.
- No diagnosis or individual medical advice; for medicines say "talk to a doctor or pharmacist".
- No miracle cures, no fake statistics, no guaranteed outcomes.

## Short (one per long video) — 38–50 s, vertical
- Hook text + voice from frame 1 (no logo). The single most surprising / useful idea of the episode.
- Fast visual change every 1–3 s, big word-by-word captions in the centre safe area.
- One "save this / send it to someone who…" line.
- Last sentence flows back into the first so the Short loops.
- Its own visual style, different from the long video.

## Visuals
- Every episode gets a NEW style (palette, fonts, motion language, transitions, music mood) — never the same as the last 5.
- Choose per scene what fits best: kinetic typography, 2D illustration (characters, organs, objects), stick figures,
  icons, charts/counters, timelines, maps, split screens.
- Style references come from the Pinterest reference board; recreate ideas originally, never copy a pin.
- Keep text inside safe areas; never overlap captions with graphics.

## Upload package
Title ≤ 70 chars, curiosity + keyword. Description: 2-line hook, what you'll learn, chapters, sources, 3–5 hashtags.
15–20 tags. Pinned comment with a question. Short: title ≤ 60 chars + #Shorts, link to the full episode.
